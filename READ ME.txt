SK COLLECTION MENSWEAR — DIRECT-OPEN PREVIEW

1. Extract the entire ZIP folder.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox. Keep the assets folder, app.js and styles.css together with index.html.
3. No Python, installation or server is needed for this direct-open preview.

This preview includes the seven storefront pages, product search, filters, product details, quick view, and a cart and wishlist saved in this browser. All products, sizes, stock and prices are illustrative. Imagery is AI-generated concept imagery.

IMPORTANT: This is a FRONTEND PREVIEW. It cannot register or sign in, create or track orders, manage stock in a database, use an admin dashboard, process payments, or send contact forms. Those features need the separate server version; even there, orders are local demonstrations and no payment or real order is processed. Do not enter real passwords or customer data here. Neither version is ready for real-world production use.

MOTION UPDATE: The homepage features an interactive wardrobe with animated image transitions. This is a 2D concept, not interactive 3D garment models. Reduced-motion settings are respected.

INTERACTIVE WARDROBE: Choose a piece on the rail or use the arrows. On mobile, swipe to browse. The wardrobe images are AI-generated concepts and are not photographs of the demo products.
